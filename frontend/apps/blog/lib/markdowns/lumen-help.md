# Help

New here? This page answers the questions a first evening on Lumen actually raises.
Nothing on it assumes you know anything about Hive.

## What is Lumen?

Lumen is a calmer place to read and write, built on top of the Hive blockchain.
You read and write here; what you publish is stored on Hive, which is public and
outside any one company's control.

You do **not** need to understand Hive to use Lumen.

## Do I need a wallet, a password, or keys?

No. A **Lumen account** is free and has nothing for you to save — no master
password, no keys, no seed phrase, no wallet to fund.

You prove it is you by signing in with one of these:

* **Google** — nothing else required.
* **A Bitcoin or Ethereum wallet** — you sign a short message to prove the wallet
  is yours. It is a signature, not a transaction: nothing is sent, and it costs
  nothing.
* **Hive Keychain** — this one is for people who *already have* a Hive account and
  want to use it. If that isn't you, ignore it.

Whichever you use, that is how you sign back in later. Losing access to it means
losing access to the account, so use one you intend to keep.

## Choosing a name

Your Lumen name is how you appear everywhere here, and **it cannot be changed
later**. Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes; 3–16 characters.

## What happens when I post?

1. Your post is saved on Lumen immediately, and appears **on your profile** right
   away — that is where to look for it first.
2. It is then queued to be published to Hive. That is a separate step and it is
   not instant.
3. Once it is on Hive, it can start appearing to other readers.

Lumen publishes on your behalf, so on Hive the post carries a line saying
*"Posted via Lumen by &lt;your name&gt;"*. On Lumen itself it is simply yours.

If you have just posted and cannot find it in the feeds, that is expected: feeds
are built from Hive, and your post reaches Hive a little after you write it.

## Will I earn anything?

Not on a Lumen account. Posts published through Lumen **decline all rewards** —
they earn nothing, for you or for us. This is deliberate: we would rather say so
plainly than imply earnings we are not paying.

Earning starts when you **upgrade to your own Hive account** (see below).

## What is "upgrading"?

Upgrading gives you a real Hive account of your own, with your own keys. From then
on you post directly to Hive under your own name, and your posts can earn.

On Lumen, your posts, your history and your profile all move to your new name.
Posts you wrote before upgrading stay published through Lumen's account on
chain, so other Hive sites will not list them under your new name.

Upgrading is optional, and nothing pushes you into it.

## Terms you may see elsewhere

Hive has its own vocabulary. On a Lumen account, **none of it is something you have
to manage**:

* **Resource Credits (RC)** — a Hive account's free allowance for posting,
  commenting and voting. It refills by itself. You do not need any to write on
  Lumen; the allowance used to publish your posts is ours.
  Buying and selling creator tokens is different, because that happens on Magi
  rather than on Hive. There it is your own HBD balance that pays, and sending
  the transaction still costs nothing: holding 1 HBD gives you 1,000 credits,
  spending them sets aside part of your balance for a while, and it comes back
  on its own over about five days. Adding HBD raises it again straight away. One
  purchase needs roughly 2 HBD of credit. The trade itself is not free: every
  buy and sell on the curve pays a 10% fee, and selling soon after buying adds
  an early-exit fee on top, which fades to zero over 6 weeks.
* **Keys / master password / owner key** — how a Hive account proves itself. A
  Lumen account has none: you sign in with Google or a wallet instead.
* **HP, VESTS, MVEST** — Hive's stake and voting-weight units. They belong to Hive
  accounts, not Lumen ones.

## Something looks wrong

If a post seems to have vanished, check your profile first — a post that is saved
but not yet on Hive lives there and only there. If it is not on your profile
either, it did not save, and writing it again is safe.

## I already have a Hive account

Sign in with **Hive Keychain**. Your keys stay in the extension, on your device;
Lumen never sees them. Everything above about Lumen accounts — declined rewards,
publishing through us — does not apply to you: you post to Hive directly, under
your own name, and you keep your rewards.

## The deeper Hive documentation

If you want the blockchain-level detail — key types, Resource Credits, account
creation, governance — the full Hive FAQ is at
[hive.io/faq](https://hive.io/faq.html). It is written for people running their
own Hive account, and you do not need it to use Lumen.

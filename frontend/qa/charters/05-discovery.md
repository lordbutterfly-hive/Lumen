# Charter: finding something to read

## Mission
Use every discovery surface: the feed tabs, search (header and page), topics in
the right rail, tags, and following/followers. Try to end up somewhere
interesting from a cold start.

## Risk oracles
- **Search that returns nothing useful.** Empty results, no empty state, no
  loading state, or results that ignore what you typed.
- **A surface that shows machinery.** Raw community ids, reward-token tags,
  internal ids, or anything a reader cannot interpret.
- **Follow does not stick.** Follow/unfollow that does not persist across a
  reload, or a Following feed that ignores it.
- **Dead ends from links.** Every topic/tag/author link must land somewhere real.

# Charter: who am I, and can I get back in

## Mission
Exercise identity end to end: sign up, sign out, sign back in with the same
wallet, and visit every account surface you can find (profile, settings,
security/sign-in & recovery, wallet, upgrade).

## Risk oracles
- **Locked out.** Anything that stops you returning to an account you own.
- **Wrong person.** Chrome that shows stale identity after logout, or another
  account's data on your page.
- **A scary page with no explanation.** Key/recovery/upgrade screens that show
  raw crypto material or jargon without telling a newcomer what to do.
- **Silent auth failure.** An action that quietly no-ops because the session
  was not what the UI thought it was.

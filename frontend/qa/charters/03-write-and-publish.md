# Charter: writing something and knowing it worked

## Mission
Post from the composer as a lite account. Then find what you posted, edit it if
you can, delete it, and see whether the app kept up.

## Risk oracles
- **The button lies.** "Post" must post. Anything that navigates, silently
  fails, or leaves you unsure whether it worked is a Blocker.
- **Your words go missing.** Text lost on error, on navigation, on refresh, or
  a post that never appears anywhere you look.
- **No confirmation.** Publishing is scary for a newcomer. If nothing tells you
  it worked, that is a finding even though the post exists.
- **Long text, odd text.** Try a very long post, one line, emoji, markdown,
  a URL, leading/trailing whitespace. Anything that mangles or crashes.

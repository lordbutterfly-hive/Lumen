# Charter: a day as somebody brand new

## Mission
You heard about Lumen today. Sign up, find something worth reading, write your
first post, come back later, and go and look at "Creators" because it sounded
interesting. Judge every step as a person who has never touched crypto.

Harness: `qa/harness/day-newcomer.mjs` — it carries the ordered story, both
origins, and evidence helpers. Read it first.

## Risk oracles
- **A moment you'd give up.** Anything confusing, scary, slow or unexplained
  enough that a normal person closes the tab. Name the exact moment.
- **The app tells you something untrue** — a number, a count, a promise about
  what just happened, a name that isn't yours.
- **Your first post.** Do you believe it worked, and can you find it again?
- **Coming back.** Second visit must be fast and must not re-ask anything.

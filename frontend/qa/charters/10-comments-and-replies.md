# Charter: the conversation

## Mission
Reply to posts and to other replies, as a lite account, on both Lumen-native and
ordinary Hive posts. Then read those threads back as somebody else.

## Risk oracles
- **A reply that goes nowhere.** Posted with no confirmation, attached to the
  wrong parent, or invisible after a reload.
- **Byline leaks.** A reply's author line showing a raw id, "undefined", or the
  publisher account instead of the person who wrote it.
- **Hostile input in the reply box.** Very long text, markdown, a script tag,
  RTL text, zero-width characters, only whitespace. Nothing should execute, and
  the refusal must be readable.
- **Threading.** Depth, ordering, and the "single comment thread" view — does it
  tell you where you are and let you get back to the full post?

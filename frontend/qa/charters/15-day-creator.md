# Charter: a day as someone deciding whether to sell their time here

## Mission
You write elsewhere and you are evaluating Lumen as a place to earn. Read the
creators directory, study one token, try to buy and sell it, look at a market
that is winding down, ask a creator for something, and see whether you could
launch a token yourself. Then post something as a writer.

Harness: `qa/harness/day-creator.mjs` — includes `moneyOnPage`, because two
figures that disagree is the failure that matters most here.

★ Creator tokens live on the **DEV** origin today and serve DEMO data — the Magi
testnet API is down. Judge flows and copy there, never speed, and never report a
demo figure as real market data. Everything else stays on the production origin.

★ A LITE account cannot sign transactions, so it cannot actually trade. That is
the true current state, not a bug — judge how HONESTLY the app says so, and
whether you understood it before you tried.

## Risk oracles
- **Two numbers that disagree** about the same thing, anywhere on any page.
- **Money you cannot explain.** A price, fee, cap, floor or payout you cannot
  work out from what the page tells you.
- **A promise with no mechanism** — anything the copy says you will get that the
  interface gives you no way to obtain or verify.
- **The moment you'd stop trusting it.** Name it precisely.

# Charter: creator tokens and the studio

## Mission
Walk everything under /creators — the directory, a token page, the studio and
the launch flow — as someone curious about launching their own token. Nobody
has driven this area yet.

## Risk oracles
- **Chain jargon with no explanation.** Contract ids, wei, gas, "vsc1…" strings,
  anything a non-crypto reader cannot act on.
- **A dead or lying control.** Buttons that do nothing, forms that accept input
  and then fail, numbers presented as live that are placeholders.
- **No honest unavailable state.** Where the feature genuinely is not deployed,
  the app must say so plainly (it does this well elsewhere) rather than erroring
  or showing an empty shell.
- **Money shown wrong.** Any balance, price or supply that is unformatted,
  mis-rounded, or inconsistent between two places on screen.

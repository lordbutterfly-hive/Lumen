# Charter: reading, and finding your way back

## Mission
Read Lumen the way a reader does: open posts from the feed, follow authors into
their profiles, use the tabs and the rails, and get back to where you started.

## Risk oracles
- **A dead end.** Any page you can reach but not leave, or that drops you
  somewhere unrelated when you go back.
- **A door to somewhere else.** Anything that looks like Lumen but is the old
  Hive frontend underneath, or that links off to it.
- **A number that lies.** Counts, payouts, follower/post totals, dates and
  ages — check they agree with the thing they label.
- **Content that never arrives.** Skeletons that never resolve, images that
  never load, tabs that stay empty when they should not be.

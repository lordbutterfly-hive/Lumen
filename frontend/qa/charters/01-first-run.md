# Charter: the first five minutes

## Mission
Arrive as somebody who has never heard of Hive, make an account, and get to a
feed you would actually want to read. Then close the tab and come back.

## Risk oracles
- **The wait is unexplained.** Building a first ranked feed costs seconds. If
  the app ever makes you wait without saying it is working, that is a finding.
- **The feed is nobody's.** After picking interests, the feed should visibly
  reflect them. If it looks like a generic list, say so and show why.
- **The second visit costs what the first did.** Coming back must be instant and
  must not re-ask anything already answered.
- **You cannot tell you are signed in.** Identity should be obvious and stable
  from the moment you land.
